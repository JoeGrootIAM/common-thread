# Common Thread

**Build a business that supports your community-and your life.**

[Live demo](https://JoeGrootIAM.github.io/common-thread/)

Common Thread helps people turn their lived experience into small, locally rooted offers that meet real needs nearby. It is intentionally values-driven and non-extractive: the goal is useful work, fair livelihoods, and stronger communities-not growth at all costs.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to create productive, meaningful work that benefits both themselves and the people around them. No formal business background required. Especially useful for people who:

- Have practical skills, care experience, or local knowledge
- Want to start small and test ideas with real neighbors
- Prefer human-centered, low-overhead approaches over scale-first models
- Care about keeping value rooted in their community

## Core principles

Before any suggestion is made, Common Thread holds these commitments:

1. **Listen before building** - Ask the people affected what they need and what already exists.
2. **Suggestions are hypotheses** - A match is a starting point, not proof of demand or impact.
3. **Protect people and place** - Respect consent, capacity, local rules, and fair pay.
4. **Grow only if it helps** - Choose durable, useful work over extraction or endless expansion.

A business is one possible response-not the answer to every community need.

## How it works

The tool walks you through three simple steps:

1. **Your hands and mind** - Inventory the skills, experience, tools, and capacity you already have.
2. **Your neighborhood** - Identify needs you've actually observed or heard about (not assumption).
3. **Your first offer** - Generate a grounded, small-scale offer with clear next steps, a light visualization path, and a basic sustainability check.

Everything stays on the page. Nothing is sent or saved by the demo.

## Knowledge commons

The site also points to practical, publicly available starting points for localization, repair culture, community growing, lean planning, and basic legal/tax resources-so people can keep learning and acting locally.

## Contributing

Common Thread is meant to grow through shared intelligence-human and AI working together.

Ways to contribute:

- **Improve the offer catalog** - Suggest new grounded, community-serving ideas that fit the principles above.
- **Strengthen the matching logic** - Help make suggestions more accurate and locally relevant.
- **Clarify the language** - Make instructions clearer, more accessible or better translated.
- **Add local context** - Share region-specific resources, constraints, or examples that help others.

Open an issue or pull request. When suggesting a new idea, please include:
- The community need it addresses
- The skills or lived experience it draws on
- Why it can stay small, local, and non-extractive
- Any important safety, legal, or capacity notes

## Philosophy in one sentence

The healthiest communities can feed, build, fix, and care for themselves. Useful work that strengthens those capacities is worth doing.
